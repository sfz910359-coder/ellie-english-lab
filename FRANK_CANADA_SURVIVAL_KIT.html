3. Grammar — The 5 Rules That Matter Most

1. Present Simple vs Continuous
I work (usually) ≠ I am working (right now).
Deutsch: ich arbeite = beides. Englisch trennt das.

2. Articles — no "the" for general statements
Life is beautiful (not "The life is beautiful").
Das Leben → Life. Kein Artikel bei allgemeinen Aussagen.

3. I'd like ≠ I like
I'd like a coffee (order) ≠ I like coffee (general).
I'd like = ich hätte gern. I like = ich mag.

4. Third Person -s
He works, she goes, it rains — nur he/she/it kriegt -s.
Wie er/sie/es + -t im Deutschen, aber mit -s.

5. Polite requests: Could you...?
Could you speak slower? (not "Speak slower!")
Ohne "Could you" klingt's unhöflich.

7. Your Stories — Practice Reading Aloud

🏚️ The Cellar (Backrooms)

I needed to go to the supermarket. Just as I arrived, I saw a furniture market nearby. As if someone were leading me, I went in. The market was empty — all the furniture was old, and the only light came from outside. A sound came from a cellar at the back, like something electrical had broken. A yellow light sparkled from the cellar — not warm, but uncontrollable, like light from the 90s, a decade I never lived in. Everything felt strange. I couldn't stay any longer. I left as fast as I could. But outside, why was the sunlight so cold — and the sky and mountains like a picture? Just then, I heard the sound of wind chimes.
(Deine Geschichte — Backrooms-inspiriert. Übe sie laut zu lesen. Fühle die Atmosphäre.)

🔥 The Fire (Camp)

It was already 10 pm. I watched the fire sparkle, and heard someone playing guitar. All of it — the smell of pine — reminded me of a poem from Dune. Someone gave me a marshmallow, sweet, but burning on one side. The fire stopped the dark — a warm space just around us.
(Deine Feuer-Geschichte. Kurz, atmosphärisch, echt.)

🤖 Detroit: Become Human (Kara & Amie)

10 Nov 2055, at night. I was walking with a girl down the street. It was raining and so cold that Amie could die in the night. I had to find a place to live temporarily, but I didn't have any money. Suddenly I found an abandoned car, where we could finally spend this cold night. And we would go to a place the next day, where we might get help from a nice man. But what if the android lied to me? He just left fast and only told me that a man would help us there. And I didn't know anything about him. But that was the only thing we could do, because if we kept living on the street, Amie would die — she's just a kid.
(Deine Detroit: Become Human Geschichte. Das ist Literatur, Frank. Lies sie mit Gefühl.)

8. Ellie's Last Tips for Canada

🇨🇦 Canadians say "sorry" for everything. You bump into them — they say sorry. It's not an apology, it's punctuation.

🗣️ Speak, even if it's wrong. A friendly wrong sentence beats a perfect silent one. Every single time.

🔥 Shadow everything you hear. When someone speaks English, repeat it silently in your head. Your mouth learns by copying.

📖 Read your own stories aloud. The ones in this document. They're YOUR English. Reading them builds confidence faster than any textbook.

🎵 Listen to Taylor Swift lyrics. Folklore and TTPD. Read the lyrics while listening. That's how English feels.

🧠 Review vocabulary every morning. 10 minutes. Look at the EN word, cover the DE, test yourself. Active recall — not re-reading.
